\section{Conclusion}

We evaluated whether one-sided SOAP preconditioning can improve a
Moonlight-Muon-style update under a controlled 200M-token Transformer
pretraining budget. Selectively applying the preconditioner to both MLP
projection matrices reduced the prespecified validation-NLL trajectory area in
all five held-out paired seeds. The final validation NLL was mixed, while the
treatment reduced training throughput by 1.70\% and increased peak memory by
0.055~GiB. Within this setting, the evidence therefore supports a modest
trajectory-level sample-efficiency benefit with a measurable systems cost, not
a general or final-quality claim. The next empirical step is a separately
planned fresh-seed study of the preconditioner refresh/cost trade-off and
mechanism diagnostics; it should not reuse the present confirmation seeds for
tuning.
