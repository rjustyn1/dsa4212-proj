\section{Conclusion}

We evaluated whether one-sided SOAP preconditioning can improve a
Moonlight-Muon-style update under a controlled 200M-token Transformer
pretraining budget. Selectively applying the preconditioner to both MLP
projection matrices reduced the prespecified validation-NLL trajectory area in
all five held-out paired seeds. That area difference is concentrated early,
while the late mean curve and final validation NLL are mixed. The treatment is
slower at the shared NLL $\leq4.20$ wall-clock crossing in all five pairs,
reduces training throughput by 1.70\%, and increases peak memory by 0.055~GiB.
Within this setting, the evidence therefore supports an early token-indexed
trajectory difference with a measurable systems cost, not a general,
final-quality, or wall-clock claim. The next empirical step is an
equal-budget learning-rate screen that extends the Moonlight Muon range, then
a fresh-seed confirmation if its selection changes; it should not reuse the
present confirmation seeds for tuning.
