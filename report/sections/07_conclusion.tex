\section{Conclusion}

We evaluated whether one-sided SOAP preconditioning can improve a
Moonlight-Muon-style update under a controlled 200M-token Transformer
pretraining budget. A matched shared-rate screen selected
$4.8\times10^{-3}$ for both methods, and a fresh five-seed paired confirmation
then tested that frozen configuration. SOAP--Muon had lower sampled trajectory
area in four pairs, but the mean difference was small and its bootstrap interval
crossed zero. The mean trajectory reversed after 80M tokens, final validation
NLL was higher on average, and the treatment reduced throughput by 1.67\% while
increasing peak memory by 0.055~GiB. Within this setting, one-sided SOAP
preconditioning therefore does not provide a reliable early-training,
final-quality, or wall-clock improvement over properly screened Moonlight
Muon. The data instead demonstrate the importance of separating one-seed
configuration selection from fresh paired confirmation.
