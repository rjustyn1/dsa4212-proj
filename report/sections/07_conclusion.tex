\section{Conclusion}

We tested whether adding SOAP's rescaling step to the MLP layers improves
Moonlight Muon when training a small language model on 200M tokens. With both
optimizers tuned in the same way and tested on five new seeds, SOAP--Muon
learned faster during the first 40M tokens, but its lead faded by about 80M
tokens. Its average loss over training was essentially the same as the
baseline's ($-0.034\%$), its final loss was slightly higher for every seed, and
it trained 2.37\% more slowly. An earlier, less carefully tuned comparison had
favored SOAP--Muon, and repeating a single run changed the result by almost as
much as the effect itself. In this setting, SOAP--Muon therefore does not
improve on a well-tuned Moonlight Muon. More broadly, our results show that a
fair comparison of optimizers needs equal tuning, new seeds for testing, and a
look at the whole training curve rather than a single point on it.
