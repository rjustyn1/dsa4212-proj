\section{Conclusion}

We evaluated whether one-sided SOAP preconditioning can improve a
Moonlight-Muon-style update under a controlled 200M-token Transformer
pretraining budget. Relative to the initially selected baseline, selectively
applying the preconditioner to both MLP projection matrices reduced the
prespecified validation-NLL trajectory area in all five held-out paired seeds.
That area difference is concentrated early, while the late mean curve and final
validation NLL are mixed; the treatment is also slower at the shared NLL
$\leq4.20$ wall-clock crossing in all five pairs, reduces training throughput
by 1.70\%, and increases peak memory by 0.055~GiB. Crucially, a subsequent
baseline-only learning-rate extension produced a lower screening area at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$ than any candidate in the initial screen. The initial
baseline was under-tuned, so these paired runs do not establish that SOAP--Muon
improves a properly screened Moonlight Muon update. The next empirical step is
a fresh equal-budget screen for both methods, followed by a fresh-seed paired
confirmation; the present confirmation seeds must not be reused for tuning.
