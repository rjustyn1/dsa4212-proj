\section{Conclusion}

We evaluated whether one-sided SOAP preconditioning can improve a
Moonlight-Muon-style update under a controlled 200M-token Transformer
pretraining budget. A matched shared-rate screen selected
$4.8\times10^{-3}$ for both methods, and a fresh five-seed paired confirmation
then tested that frozen configuration. SOAP--Muon had lower sampled trajectory
area in four pairs, but the mean difference was small and its bootstrap interval
crossed zero. SOAP--Muon led in every pair for the first 40M tokens, but the
mean lead was gone by about 80M tokens, so the prespecified full-trajectory
area shows no reliable advantage. Final validation NLL was higher in every
pair, and the treatment reduced throughput by 2.37\% while increasing peak
memory by 0.055~GiB. Within this setting, one-sided SOAP preconditioning
therefore provides an early lead but no reliable full-trajectory, final-quality,
or wall-clock improvement over properly screened Moonlight Muon. The data
instead demonstrate the importance of separating one-seed configuration
selection from fresh paired confirmation.
