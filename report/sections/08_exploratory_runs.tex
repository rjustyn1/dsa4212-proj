\section{Tuning Runs and Early Experiments}
\label{app:exploratory}

This appendix describes runs that shaped the implementation and settings but
are not part of the main experiment.

\subsection*{Tuning runs}

In the first round (Table~\ref{tab:screening-record}), SOAP--Muon on both MLP
matrices at $1.2\times10^{-3}$ had a curve area of 2.610, against 2.636 for
Moonlight Muon at the same rate. Looking back, this gap of 0.026 was much
smaller than the 0.141 improvement the baseline gained from its last doubling
of the learning rate, from $6\times10^{-4}$ to $1.2\times10^{-3}$, a sign that
the baseline needed larger rates. Trying larger rates lowered the baseline's
area to 2.518 at $4.8\times10^{-3}$. In the second round
(Table~\ref{tab:shared-rate-screen}), both optimizers did best at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$ (2.518 for the baseline and 2.507 for SOAP--Muon) and worse
at $2.4\times10^{-3}$ and $9.6\times10^{-3}$. SOAP--Muon had the lower area at
every rate, which is consistent with its early lead in the main experiment,
because these short runs mostly measure early training.
