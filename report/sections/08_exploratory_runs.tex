\section{Exploratory Runs and Tuning Record}

This appendix records runs that informed implementation and configuration but
are not part of the five-seed confirmation result. They are included to make
the search history and its limitations inspectable, not to strengthen the
confirmatory claim.

Table~\ref{tab:screening-record} gives the complete seed-101, 40M-token
selection screen. The frozen treatment (both MLP projections at
$1.2\times10^{-3}$) has area 2.610, compared with 2.636 for Moonlight Muon at
the same learning rate. This 0.026 difference is smaller than the 0.141 gain
Moonlight Muon obtained from its last tested learning-rate doubling, from
$6\times10^{-4}$ to $1.2\times10^{-3}$. A subsequent extension of the
Moonlight Muon screen finds areas 2.549 at $2.4\times10^{-3}$ and 2.518 at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$, both lower than the selected SOAP--Muon value. The
original baseline grid was therefore under-tuned. These added points do not
alter the historical confirmation metrics, but they invalidate an optimizer
advantage claim based on that frozen baseline. A fresh five-seed paired
confirmation against the $4.8\times10^{-3}$ baseline is required before
making a comparative effectiveness claim.
