\section{Exploratory Runs and Tuning Record}

This appendix records runs that informed implementation and configuration but
are not part of the five-seed confirmation result. They are included to make
the search history and its limitations inspectable, not to strengthen the
confirmatory claim.

Table~\ref{tab:screening-record} records the initial seed-101, 40M-token
screen and the unmatched Moonlight-Muon extension. The frozen treatment (both
MLP projections at $1.2\times10^{-3}$) has area 2.610, compared with 2.636
for Moonlight Muon at the same learning rate. This 0.026 difference is smaller
than the 0.141 gain Moonlight Muon obtained from its last tested learning-rate
doubling, from $6\times10^{-4}$ to $1.2\times10^{-3}$. The subsequent
Moonlight-Muon extension reaches 2.518 at $4.8\times10^{-3}$, exposing that
the original baseline grid was under-tuned.

To repair that imbalance, Table~\ref{tab:shared-rate-screen} gives the
post-confirmation, matched shared-rate extension. Every eligible Muon, SOAP,
and AdamW parameter group uses the displayed rate, and SOAP--Muon
preconditions both MLP projections. Both methods are locally bracketed at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$: Moonlight Muon worsens from 2.518 to 2.544 at
$9.6\times10^{-3}$, while SOAP--Muon worsens from 2.507 to 2.526. SOAP--Muon
has the lower area at all three shared rates. This is nevertheless one
selection seed, run after the original confirmation; it motivates a fresh
held-out paired confirmation but does not itself establish a comparative
effectiveness claim.

Table~\ref{tab:historical-pilots} preserves the earlier AdamW, reference-style
one-sided SOAP--Muon, and Muon scaling pilots. The AdamW and reference-style
SOAP--Muon figures are broadly useful context, but their settings and seed
coverage differ from the frozen comparison, so they cannot establish a ranking
against Moonlight Muon. The early Muon result used a superseded scaling
implementation; its retained record makes the correction auditable rather
than silently dropping an unfavourable run.
