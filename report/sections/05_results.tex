\section{Results}

\subsection{Trajectory-level sample efficiency}

Figure~\ref{fig:nll-difference} shows the mean paired validation-NLL difference
and its min--max band across the five fresh held-out seed pairs. The mean
SOAP--Muon NLL is lower through 80M tokens, then higher at every logged
checkpoint from 90M tokens onward. We quantify the sampled trajectory using
the prespecified token-normalised trapezoidal area. SOAP--Muon has lower area
in four of five pairs. Its mean paired area difference is $-0.0023$, or
$-0.057\%$ relative to the Moonlight Muon mean; the deterministic bootstrap
95\% interval is $[-0.0059,+0.0015]$, the descriptive paired-$t$ 95\%
interval is $[-0.0084,+0.0038]$, and the exact two-sided sign-test $p$-value is
0.3750. The trajectory result is therefore compatible with both a small early
advantage and no reliable effect at this sample size.

\subsection{Final NLL and systems trade-off}

The full 5M-token endpoint evaluation disfavors SOAP--Muon. It has higher
final NLL for seeds 301--304 and lower final NLL only for seed 305
(Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-results}). The mean paired final-NLL difference
is $+0.0055$; its bootstrap 95\% interval, $[+0.0016,+0.0091]$, is positive,
although the descriptive paired-$t$ 95\% interval,
$[-0.0007,+0.0117]$, spans zero. The evidence does not support a final-quality
improvement at the fixed 200M-token budget.

Both methods first reached NLL $\leq 4.25$ at 100M tokens for every seed. At
NLL $\leq 4.20$, Moonlight Muon reached the threshold one evaluation earlier
for seed 301 and the methods tied for the other four seeds. These threshold
observations are secondary summaries and do not replace the primary curve-area
endpoint.

Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-wall-clock} reports elapsed wall-clock time at the
same first logged crossings, including validation. SOAP--Muon is slower at NLL
$\leq4.25$ in four pairs and tied in one. At NLL $\leq4.20$, SOAP--Muon is
slower in four pairs and faster by 0.9 seconds in one. Thus, the early
token-indexed difference does not establish a wall-clock advantage.

The mean training throughput was 96,927 tokens/s for SOAP--Muon and 98,570
tokens/s for Moonlight Muon, a 1.67\% reduction. Mean peak training-device
memory was 11.509~GiB for SOAP--Muon and 11.454~GiB for Moonlight Muon, an
increase of 0.055~GiB. All ten fresh confirmation runs completed with finite
metrics. Hence the unconfirmed trajectory difference is accompanied by a small
but measurable systems cost.

\subsection{Matched shared-rate selection}

The route and learning-rate screen that selected both MLP projections and the
$1.2\times10^{-3}$ learning rate was performed before the confirmation runs
and on a separate seed. It defines the initial treatment and comparison
settings, not additional confirmation evidence. The initial Moonlight-Muon
screen tested three learning rates from $3\times10^{-4}$ to
$1.2\times10^{-3}$ and selected its upper boundary, whereas the SOAP--Muon
screen varied only the routed MLP rate, holding AdamW and the attention
matrices at $1.2\times10^{-3}$.

After an initial five-seed comparison at the formerly selected rate, we first
extended only the Moonlight-Muon screen on the original seed and 40M-token
budget. Its area reaches 2.518 at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$ (Table~\ref{tab:screening-record}), establishing that the
initial baseline was under-tuned. We then repaired the screening imbalance with
a matched shared-rate screen at $2.4\times10^{-3}$, $4.8\times10^{-3}$, and
$9.6\times10^{-3}$. In this follow-up, every eligible Muon, SOAP, and AdamW
group uses the candidate rate. Both methods are locally bracketed at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$, and SOAP--Muon has lower area at all four shared rates:
2.610 versus 2.636, 2.537 versus 2.549, 2.507 versus 2.518, and 2.526 versus
2.544, respectively (Table~\ref{tab:shared-rate-screen}).

The matched screen is a single-seed configuration-selection result and does
not itself prove an optimizer advantage. It does, however, remove the
unequal-rate-screening objection and defines the frozen $4.8\times10^{-3}$
configuration used by the fresh seeds 301--305 confirmation above. No claim
about a routing ablation, another architecture, a different training budget,
or large-scale language-model pretraining is made from these results.
