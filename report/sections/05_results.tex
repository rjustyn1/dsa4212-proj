\subsection{Trajectory-level sample efficiency}

Figure~\ref{fig:nll-difference} shows the mean paired validation-NLL difference
and its min--max band across the five held-out seed pairs. The mean SOAP--Muon
NLL is lower through
120M tokens, then slightly higher at every logged checkpoint from 130M tokens
onward. We quantify the sampled trajectory using the prespecified
token-normalised trapezoidal area. SOAP--Muon has lower area in all five pairs.
Its mean paired area difference is $-0.0060$, or $-0.145\%$ relative to the
Moonlight Muon mean; the deterministic bootstrap 95\% interval is
$[-0.0080,-0.0040]$, the descriptive paired-$t$ 95\% interval is
$[-0.0093,-0.0027]$, and the exact two-sided sign-test $p$-value is 0.0625.
The late positive mean is driven by seeds 203 and 204, whose SOAP--Muon curve
is higher at every checkpoint from 130M tokens onward; the curves for seeds
201, 202, and 205 remain lower throughout. These are also the two seeds for
which SOAP--Muon has higher final NLL.

\subsection{Final NLL and systems trade-off}

The full 5M-token endpoint evaluation does not show a consistent advantage.
SOAP--Muon has lower final NLL for seeds 201, 202, and 205, while Moonlight
Muon has lower final NLL for seeds 203 and 204 (Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-results}).
The mean paired final-NLL difference is $+0.0009$ and its bootstrap 95\%
interval, $[-0.0014,+0.0034]$, crosses zero. The evidence therefore does
not support a final-quality improvement at the fixed 200M-token budget. The
descriptive paired-$t$ 95\% interval, $[-0.0028,+0.0047]$, also spans zero.

SOAP--Muon reached the curve threshold NLL $\leq 4.25$ at 100M tokens for all
five seeds; Moonlight Muon reached it at 100M tokens for four seeds and at
110M tokens for seed 202. Both methods first reached NLL $\leq 4.20$ at 120M
tokens for every seed under the 10M-token evaluation cadence. These threshold
observations are secondary summaries and do not replace the primary curve-area
endpoint.

Table~\ref{tab:confirmation-wall-clock} reports elapsed wall-clock time at the
same first logged crossings, including validation. SOAP--Muon is slower at NLL
$\leq4.25$ in four of five pairs; seed 202 is faster only because it reaches
the threshold one 10M-token evaluation earlier. At NLL $\leq4.20$, both methods
cross at 120M tokens in every pair and SOAP--Muon is slower in all five pairs.
Thus, the token-indexed early advantage does not establish a wall-clock
advantage.

The mean training throughput was 97,420 tokens/s for SOAP--Muon and 99,109
tokens/s for Moonlight Muon, a 1.70\% reduction. Mean peak training-device
memory was 11.509~GiB for SOAP--Muon and 11.454~GiB for Moonlight Muon, an
increase of 0.055~GiB. All ten confirmation runs completed with finite
metrics. Hence the observed trajectory difference is accompanied by a small
but measurable systems cost.

\subsection{Post-confirmation tuning audit}

The route and learning-rate screen that selected both MLP projections and the
$1.2\times10^{-3}$ learning rate was performed before the confirmation runs
and on a separate seed. It defines the initial treatment and comparison
settings, not additional confirmation evidence. The initial Moonlight-Muon
screen tested three learning rates from $3\times10^{-4}$ to
$1.2\times10^{-3}$ and selected its upper boundary, whereas SOAP--Muon tested
three routes at three learning rates, including $2.4\times10^{-3}$.

After the five-seed confirmation, we extended only the Moonlight-Muon screen
on the original seed and 40M-token budget. Its area falls from 2.636 at
$1.2\times10^{-3}$ to 2.549 at $2.4\times10^{-3}$ and 2.518 at
$4.8\times10^{-3}$ (Table~\ref{tab:screening-record}); 2.518 is lower than
the selected SOAP--Muon screen value of 2.610. This establishes that the
initial baseline was under-tuned. It does not retrospectively change the
pre-registered five-seed metrics, but it means they cannot support a claim
that SOAP preconditioning improves a properly screened Moonlight Muon
baseline. A fresh, equal-budget screen for both methods followed by a fresh
paired confirmation is required. No claim about a routing ablation, another
architecture, a different training budget, or large-scale language-model
pretraining is made from these results.
