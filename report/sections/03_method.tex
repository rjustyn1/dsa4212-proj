\section{Method}

\subsection{Moonlight Muon baseline}

The primary baseline is unpreconditioned Moonlight Muon, not an earlier Muon
variant. For an eligible dense matrix $W\in\mathbb{R}^{m\times n}$ with
gradient $G_t$, the implemented update is
\begin{align}
M_t &= 0.95M_{t-1}+G_t, \\
H_t &= G_t+0.95M_t, \\
W_t &= (1-\eta_t\lambda)W_{t-1}
      -\eta_t\,0.2\sqrt{\max(m,n)}\operatorname{NS}_5(H_t),
\end{align}
where $\eta_t$ is the scheduled learning rate and $\lambda$ is decoupled
weight decay. The quintic Newton--Schulz map first normalises
$X_0=H_t/(\lVert H_t\rVert_F+10^{-7})$ and, after transposing tall matrices,
iterates $X_{k+1}=aX_k+bP_kX_k+cP_k^2X_k$ for $P_k=X_kX_k^\top$ and
$(a,b,c)=(3.4445,-4.7750,2.0315)$. It is transposed back after five iterations.
The hidden-matrix scope and Newton--Schulz construction follow the Muon
technical specification \cite{jordan2024muon}; the RMS-matched
$0.2\sqrt{\max(m,n)}$ scaling is the Moonlight convention
\cite{liu2025muon}.


\subsection{Selective one-sided SOAP--Muon}

The treatment preserves the Muon momentum, Newton--Schulz iteration, RMS
matching, and decoupled-decay order. Its only intended algorithmic difference
is a one-sided SOAP preconditioning stage before the Muon-aligned update. For
a selected matrix, let $Q_t$ be the basis on its smaller dimension and write
$\widehat H_t$ for the corresponding one-sided projection of $H_t$. The
implemented state updates are
\begin{align}
C_t &= 0.95C_{t-1}+0.05\operatorname{Gram}(G_t), \\
\widehat H_t &= \operatorname{project}(H_t,Q_t), \\
V_t &= 0.95V_{t-1}+0.05(\widehat H_t\odot\widehat H_t), \\
U_t &= \operatorname{NS}_5\!\left(
 \widehat H_t\oslash\left(\sqrt{V_t/(1-0.95^t)}+10^{-8}\right)\right).
\end{align}
The update in the first equation uses the raw gradient, whereas the
coordinate-wise second moment uses the projected Nesterov input. $Q_1$ is
initialised by an eigendecomposition of $C_1$; every ten updates it is refreshed
by one covariance power iteration followed by QR factorisation, while $V_t$ is
reordered with the estimated eigenvalues. The returned update is
$\operatorname{projectBack}(U_t,Q_t)$, followed by the same Moonlight scale
and decoupled-decay step as the baseline.

This choice is motivated by the one-sided SOAP construction, which reduces the
state and matrix-operation cost of preconditioning rectangular matrices by
retaining the eigenbasis on only one side \cite{vyas2025soap}. It is not a
full reproduction of SOAP: the post-preconditioning update remains Muon-like
rather than Adam-like. We therefore describe the treatment as selective
one-sided SOAP--Muon rather than as SOAP itself. A rotation alone is equivariant
under the polar-style Newton--Schulz map; the treatment's substantive change is
the coordinate-wise normalisation by $V_t$ in that rotating basis.

\subsection{Parameter routing}

Only dense two-dimensional hidden matrices were candidates for a matrix-aware
optimizer. Embeddings, the tied language-model head, and all scalar or vector
parameters were assigned to AdamW. Matrices with aspect ratio at most two were
assigned to Muon. For matrices with aspect ratio between two and eight, the
SOAP--Muon treatment was assigned only to MLP \texttt{up\_proj} and
\texttt{down\_proj} weights; other eligible matrices, including attention
weights, remained on Muon. Matrices more rectangular than this limit would
remain on AdamW; none occur among the eligible hidden matrices here.

The model has 33,664,128 trainable parameters. AdamW covers 19,508,352
(58.0\%), chiefly the tied token/output embedding and position embedding;
Moonlight Muon covers 4,718,592 (14.0\%) attention matrices; and the selective
SOAP--Muon route covers 9,437,184 (28.0\%) MLP matrices.

The unpreconditioned baseline used the same routing boundary, except that all
eligible hidden matrices assigned to a matrix-aware route received Moonlight
Muon. Consequently, the controlled comparison changes the preconditioning
stage for the selected MLP projections while retaining a common auxiliary
optimizer and common treatment of the remaining hidden matrices. The frozen
