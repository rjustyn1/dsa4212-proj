\section{Discussion and Limitations}

The confirmation study supports a narrow conclusion: in this fixed setting,
selective one-sided SOAP preconditioning improves the complete validation-NLL
trajectory of the Moonlight Muon baseline. The effect is small in magnitude
but has the same direction in all five paired primary-endpoint contrasts. At
the same time, it does not translate into a reliable advantage in the final
full-validation NLL at the 200M-token budget. This distinction matters because
a lower final NLL at one selected budget and a lower learning-curve area answer
different questions. The former is mixed here, whereas the latter consistently
favours the treatment.

The improvement is also not free. SOAP--Muon has lower measured training
throughput and slightly higher peak device memory. The paper therefore treats
the result as a sample-efficiency--systems-cost trade-off, rather than an
unconditional optimizer improvement. A practitioner operating under a fixed
token budget may value the lower trajectory; a practitioner constrained by
wall-clock time should account for the measured overhead.

Several limitations bound this interpretation. First, the model is a compact
8-layer Transformer trained on one fixed corpus regime and one A10 GPU. The
result does not establish that the same trade-off holds for larger models,
other architectures, distributed training, or longer token budgets. Second,
the selected SOAP route and learning rate came from a separate but single-seed
screen. Holding the five confirmation seeds out prevents them from becoming
tuning data, but it does not make the screening result a multi-seed routing
ablation. Third, the 10M-token curve cadence limits the resolution of the two
secondary threshold metrics.

Finally, this experiment is designed to evaluate an outcome, not to prove a
mechanism. It does not contain a completed basis-age, covariance-spectrum, or
update-alignment diagnostic, nor does it compare every possible routing or
preconditioner-refresh choice. Consequently, the data do not justify an
attribution of the trajectory benefit to a particular spectral or curvature
effect. A future study should preregister such diagnostics or a fresh-seed
refresh-frequency ablation before using them to explain the observed effect.
